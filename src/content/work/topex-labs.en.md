## The situation

TOPEX Labs is a Costa Rica-based optical lens laboratory manufacturing custom prescription lenses for optometrists and optic shops across the country. The company ran a modern tech stack — SAP Business One for transactional data, Bitrix CRM mid-implementation, Wolkvox for contact center and WhatsApp — but the systems weren't connected to each other, and almost no one in the organization was extracting value from the data already inside them.

The CEO had been exploring AI tools on his own, but each department was operating independently, data lived in silos, and there was no adoption strategy, no shared data model, and no cross-departmental view of where the real bottlenecks were. Rather than proposing tools before understanding the problems, the engagement was structured as a diagnostic-first AI consultation: interview every department, map the actual workflow pain points, and deliver a grounded roadmap.

## What we did

Ran a kickoff strategy session with the CEO to align on scope and establish a three-level AI maturity framework — tool adoption, workflow automation, autonomous agents — then designed and ran six structured departmental interviews across Sales, Finance, Operations, Engineering, Marketing, and Customer Service.

Synthesizing the six conversations surfaced three cross-cutting structural problems that no single department could see from inside its own function: a data bottleneck routed through the company's one IT resource holding the only SAP license, high-value time lost to manual data consolidation (Finance alone spending an hour a day before any analysis could start), and inconsistent customer-facing communication running on unstructured WhatsApp threads with no ticketing or audit trail.

Delivered a phased Phase 1 roadmap prioritizing Customer Service and Sales — the two tracks with the clearest ROI case and the existing infrastructure to support fast implementation — architected entirely on platforms TOPEX already owned: Wolkvox for the WhatsApp and chatbot layer, Bitrix for case management and a 360° customer view, SAP for transactional data. No new platforms, no new tools to learn.

While the roadmap was being scoped, delivered five department-specific prompt training workshops immediately, plus a standalone reference guide with optimized prompts across functions, so every team had a working AI skill they could apply the next day instead of waiting for Phase 1 to ship.

## The outcome

Leadership got its first cross-functional view of where the organization's data and process gaps actually sat. The roadmap avoided vendor lock-in by building entirely on existing infrastructure, and every department gained working AI capability before a single line of Phase 1 was implemented. Projected Phase 1 ROI: $1,400–$2,400/month in recovered time, with a 3–5 month payback on the proposed investment.
